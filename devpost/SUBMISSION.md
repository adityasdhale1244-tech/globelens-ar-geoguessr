## Description (draft; edit to match the shipped build)
GlobeLens Quest turns geography into a quick, seated VR challenge. Each round places the player in a curated destination scene and offers a short visual clue. The player points to a location on an in-world map, locks in the guess, and sees the answer, distance, and score. Three compact rounds make it easy to start, finish, and replay without room-scale movement or a long tutorial.

The experience is designed for hands-first interaction: map selection and game actions are reachable from a comfortable seated position, with controller support available as a fallback. The game uses bundled scenes and round data so it can be played without a network connection. Its focus is the game itself—not a wrapper around a third-party street-view service.

For this competition, we are adapting the GeoGuessr concept into a native Meta Quest experience, with a seated VR interface, hand-first map interaction, timed rounds, score reveals, and a replayable loop. We will list only features that are present and tested in the submitted build. Future improvements could add more licensed destinations, accessibility options, and authored clue packs.
