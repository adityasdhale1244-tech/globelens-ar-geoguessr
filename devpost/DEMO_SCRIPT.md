# Demo video script (target: 75–110 seconds; must remain under 3 minutes)

**0–8 seconds — Hook**  
"Can you identify a place from one scene and a few clues? This is GlobeLens Quest, a hands-first geography game for Meta Quest."

**8–25 seconds — Show the experience**  
Show the player seated, the location scene, clue, timer, and map. Explain that all essential controls are within reach.

**25–48 seconds — Play a round**  
Use the actual hand interaction to place a map pin and submit the guess. Show the real in-headset response, not an edited mockup.

**48–70 seconds — Reveal and continue**  
Show destination, distance, points, and the next round. Demonstrate pause/resume if it is implemented.

**70–90 seconds — Close**  
Show the final score/replay and explain what is original about the game and what substantial work was completed during the competition window.

Record the actual Quest build (or permitted emulator footage). Do not advertise live street view, multiplayer, GPS, or hand features unless they work in the submitted build.
